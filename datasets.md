# Datasets

## Primary: OpenNeuro ds004504

- Subjects: 88 (36 AD, 23 FTD, 29 HC)
- Binary task: 65 subjects (36 AD, 29 HC)
- Channels: 19 (standard 10-20)
- Sampling rate: 500 Hz
- Recording: Resting state, eyes closed
- Format: BIDS, preprocessed derivatives (ASR + ICA)
- Access: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds004504
- License: Free, open access

## External: FSU Dataset

- Subjects: 92 (80 AD, 12 HC)
- Channels: 19 (standard 10-20)
- Sampling rate: 128 Hz
- Recording: Resting state, eyes closed
- Note: Severe class imbalance limits interpretability

## External: ADSZ Dataset

- Subjects: 48 (24 AD, 24 HC)
- Channels: 19
- Sampling rate: 128 Hz
- Balanced classes — used for cross-dataset evaluation

## Note on Data

No raw datasets are included in this repository.  
Download from the sources above and place under data/.
